Standard & Compound Units
NUET / GCSE Higher — Theory Pack


📘  SECTION 1 — LESSON

Overview
This topic covers how we measure the physical world and how units relate to one another. It splits into two areas:
• Standard units — single quantities (mass, length, time, temperature, money) and converting between them.
• Compound units — two or more standard units combined into a rate or ratio (speed, density, pressure). The unit itself tells you the formula.
This topic appears heavily in real-life contexts: fuel costs, pay rates, recipe scaling, map reading, medicine dosages, engineering.

Part 3 — Standard Conversions
Length
• 1 km = 1 000 m = 100 000 cm = 1 000 000 mm
• 1 mile ≈ 1.609 km  (useful: 5 miles ≈ 8 km)
• 1 inch = 2.54 cm
Mass
• 1 tonne = 1 000 kg  |  1 kg = 1 000 g  |  1 g = 1 000 mg
• 1 pound (lb) ≈ 0.4536 kg  |  1 stone = 14 lb ≈ 6.35 kg
Volume
• 1 m³ = 1 000 litres = 1 000 000 cm³
• 1 litre = 1 000 ml = 1 000 cm³ = 1 dm³
• 1 UK gallon ≈ 4.546 litres
Time
• 1 min = 60 s  |  1 hour = 3 600 s  |  1 day = 86 400 s
Area
• 1 m² = 10 000 cm²  |  1 km² = 100 hectares  |  1 ha = 10 000 m²
Temperature
• °C → K: add 273.15
• °C → °F: multiply by 9/5 then add 32

Part 4 — Speed
• Average speed = total distance ÷ total time  (NOT the average of individual speeds!)
• Same direction: relative speed = difference of speeds
• Opposite directions: relative speed = sum of speeds
• Catching up: time = head-start distance ÷ speed difference
• km/h → m/s: divide by 3.6   |   m/s → km/h: multiply by 3.6

Part 5 — Density
• Object density < fluid density → floats
• Object density > fluid density → sinks
• Mixed density = (m₁ + m₂) ÷ (V₁ + V₂)  — do NOT average unless equal volumes
• 1 g/cm³ = 1 000 kg/m³
• Benchmarks: Water = 1 000  |  Aluminium = 2 700  |  Iron ≈ 7 800  |  Gold = 19 300  (kg/m³)

Part 6 — Pressure
• Hydrostatic pressure: P = ρgh  (ρ in kg/m³, g = 9.81 m/s², h = depth in m)
• Pascal's principle: F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂
• 1 Pa = 1 N/m²  |  1 kPa = 1 000 Pa  |  1 atm ≈ 101 325 Pa

Part 7 — Rates of Pay
• Earnings = hourly rate × hours worked
• Overtime: 1.5× (time and a half) or 2× (double time)
• Commission: total pay = base pay + (commission rate × sales)
• Annual = weekly pay × 52  |  Monthly = annual ÷ 12

Part 8 — Unit Pricing & Best Value
• Price per unit = total price ÷ quantity — always convert to SAME unit first
• Exchange rates: multiply to get foreign currency, divide to get back

Part 9 — Other Compound Measures
• Flow rate: Q = V ÷ t  (m³/s or litres/min)  |  two pipes: add rates
• Power: P = E ÷ t  (Watts)  |  1 kWh = 3 600 000 J = 3.6 MJ
• Fuel cost per km = (L/100km × price per litre) ÷ 100

🎯  SECTION 4 — EXAM STRATEGIES

S01	Write the formula first
Before substituting any numbers, write the formula. This prevents mistakes and earns partial marks. Example: write P = F/A → then substitute → 840 ÷ 0.0314 = ...

S02	Check units cancel correctly
Units behave like fractions. km/h × h = km ✓. If the units don't give what the question asks for, you made an error before the arithmetic.

S03	Convert EVERYTHING to consistent units first
Mixing kg with g/cm³ is the #1 source of errors. Pick one unit system and convert all values before calculating.

S04	Use the triangle trick
Draw a triangle for D/S/T, M/D/V, F/P/A. Cover the variable you want — the remaining two show the operation (multiply or divide).

S05	Average speed trap
Average speed ≠ average of speeds. Classic trap: '50 km/h there, 100 km/h back → average is NOT 75.' Always use total distance ÷ total time.

S06	Sense-check your answer
After calculating, ask: is this realistic? A cyclist at 8 000 m/s? No. A monthly bill of £0.02? No. One second of thought saves marks.

S07	Best value — always same unit
Convert all prices to the same unit (e.g. pence per 100 ml) before comparing. Never compare £/500ml with £/1.5L directly.

S08	Standard form for very large/small values
Use scientific notation to avoid miscounting zeros. 0.000 007 m = 7 × 10⁻⁶ m. Prevents silly arithmetic errors.

S09	Opposite-direction relative speed
Two objects moving toward each other (or away in opposite directions): relative speed = SUM. Same direction: DIFFERENCE.

S10	Compound unit = the formula
The unit tells you the formula. kg/m³ → mass/volume. N/m² → force/area. m/s → distance/time. Read the unit like a fraction.
